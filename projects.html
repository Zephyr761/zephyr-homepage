COURSE PROJECTOct — Dec 2025

Cassava Leaf Disease
Classification

Compared deep learning approaches to classify five categories of cassava leaf disease in natural field images.

Challenge
Class imbalance, changing light, and complex backgrounds make classification harder.
Method
Built ResNet50 and ViT training and evaluation pipelines; used transfer learning and augmentation; tested Bi-Tempered Logistic Loss.
Result
With stratified sampling and cross-validation, ResNet50 performed best overall, reaching 77.71% classification accuracy.
